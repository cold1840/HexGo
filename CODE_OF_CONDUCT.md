# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

HexGo is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical ability.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect different languages, time zones, abilities, and experience levels.
- Credit the work of others and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Protect private, personal, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, or targeted hostility;
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- Insults, trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith argument;
- Publishing another person's private information without permission;
- Impersonation, plagiarism, or deliberately misleading claims about authorship, review, or test results;
- Any conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional community.

These standards apply in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, reviews, project discussions, and public spaces where someone represents HexGo.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If it is safe to do so, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive details in a public issue. For conduct that occurs on GitHub, you may also use GitHub's blocking and abuse-reporting tools.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical, review the available context, avoid conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately. Possible actions include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code of conduct may be removed from project responsibilities by the other maintainers.

## Scope and Responsibility

Participation in HexGo means accepting this code of conduct. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing it, but every participant helps keep the community healthy.
